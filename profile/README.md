# Z Quant Research Lab

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **Z Quant Research Lab**. We are a dedicated research group focusing on the intersection of advanced quantitative modeling, decentralized systems, and artificial intelligence.

## 🚀 Our Focus Areas
* **Quantitative Finance:** Algorithmic trading strategies, statistical arbitrage, and high-frequency backtesting frameworks.
* **Blockchain & Web3:** Smart contract development, DeFi protocol design, and automated liquidity optimization.
* **Machine Learning:** Data-driven predictive models, sentiment analysis, and market forecasting.

## 💡 Technologies & Tools
* **Quantitative Analysis:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, C++
* **Decentralized Finance:** Solidity, Hardhat, Web3.js, Ethers.js
* **AI & Data Science:** TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn
